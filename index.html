Projects

Tata Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights

Conducted a comprehensive data visualization simulation analyzing 2011 retail sales data to provide actionable insights for executive decision-making. The project focused on transforming raw transactional data into clear, strategic insights for both the CEO and CMO.

Project Objective

  • Forecast revenue trends and identify seasonal patterns.
  • Identify top-performing markets and products.
  • Recognize high-value customers for targeted retention strategies.
  • Analyze global demand to guide market expansion decisions.

Methodology & Key Metrics

  • Data Cleaning & Preparation: Filtered 2011 transactions, removed invalid entries (Quantity <1, Unit Price ≤ 0), and calculated Revenue as the primary metric.
  • Revenue Trend Analysis: Line chart visualizing monthly revenue to detect seasonality and guide forecasting. Metrics: Monthly revenue, MoM growth.
  • Top Countries & Product Analysis: Side-by-side bar chart highlighting top 10 countries (excluding UK) with revenue and units sold. Metrics: Revenue per country, units sold, revenue per unit.
  • Top Customers by Revenue: Vertical bar chart ranking top 10 revenue-generating customers. Metrics: Total revenue contribution per customer.
  • Global Demand Mapping: Geographic map showing units sold by country. Metrics: Demand density per region.

This project provided a 360-degree view of sales performance, enabling executive leadership to make data-driven decisions on forecasting, market expansion, customer retention, and resource planning.
